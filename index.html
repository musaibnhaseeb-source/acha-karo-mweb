How it works

Three steps. Every small acha kaam counts.

1

Do an acha kaam

Clear litter, feed a stray, water a plant. Pick one and do it.

2

Take a photo

Photograph the acha kaam in the app and share it in seconds. Most are approved right away.

3

Get points & badges

Build streaks, unlock badges and climb the weekly board. Only your first name and last initial are shown (like “Ayesha K.”), and a nickname is fine.
