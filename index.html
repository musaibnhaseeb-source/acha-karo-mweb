Meet Guddi 🇵🇰

Guddi is the face of Acha Karo — curious, mischievous, sometimes scared, and always learning.

She wasn't always someone who stopped to help. She started out doing ordinary things, making mistakes, and learning along the way. Then she discovered that doing good can be an adventure too.

Through Guddi's journey, you'll see her explore Pakistan, discover its stories, take on challenges, help others, and learn that making a difference doesn't always require something big.

You don't have to be perfect. You just have to start.
