Guddi v1.3 Launch Bonus: Rules

To celebrate Guddi v1.3, we're giving bonus Acha Karo points to new members who do their first acha kaam. Here's exactly how it works.

What you can get

  • The first 10 new users who qualify get 100 Acha Karo points each.
  • After the campaign ends, 3 people are picked at random from everyone who qualified during the campaign. Each gets 50 bonus points.

Who can take part

  • New accounts created during the campaign window.
  • You must be 16 or older.
  • One account per person. Duplicate accounts are disqualified.

How to qualify

  1. Sign up for Acha Karo.
  2. Complete your profile.
  3. Do your first acha kaam. It must be approved by our team.

Order for the first 10 is based on the time your first deed was submitted.

Dates

The campaign runs from [START DATE] to [END DATE] (Pakistan time, 7 days).

The first-10 part ends early once 10 people qualify. Everyone who qualifies during the full 7 days is still included in the random pick.

How winners are chosen

  • The random pick uses a public random tool and is screen-recorded.
  • Winners are announced by membership number, plus first name or initials only with their permission.
  • We never share usernames, photos or cities.

About the points

  • Points are in-app only and have no cash value.
  • They can't be exchanged or transferred.
  • No purchase is needed to take part.
  • Points are added manually by the Acha Karo team after checking.
  • The Acha Karo team's decision is final.

Questions?

Reach us through the Contact Us form. Guddi reads everything.

This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with Instagram, Meta or TikTok.
