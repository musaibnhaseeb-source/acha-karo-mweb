Why isn't Acha Karo on the Play Store?

Not yet. For now you install it straight from our website (this is called an APK). Lots of new apps start this way. On iPhone, use the web version. It's the same app and works in Safari.

Android asks you once to allow Chrome to install apps from outside the Play Store. Turn it on for Chrome, install Acha Karo, then you can turn it off again (Settings → Apps → Chrome → Install unknown apps).

Tap Use on iPhone (Web) in Safari. For an app icon, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

How do I update?

Download the latest APK from this page and install it over the old one. Your account and points stay safe because they're saved to your account, not the phone.

Points

What are Acha Karo points?

Points show the acha kaam you've done. They're in-app points only, have no cash value, and no purchase is ever needed. You can't sell, swap, transfer or cash them out.

How do I earn points?

Do an acha kaam, add a photo in the app, and our team checks it. Once it's approved, your points appear on your profile and the leaderboard.

Why hasn't my acha kaam been approved yet?

A real person checks every submission, so it can take a little time. [CONFIRM: typical review time, e.g. "within 24–48 hours"] If it's rejected, you can try again with a clearer photo.

Can I lose points?

Yes, if a submission turns out to be fake, copied, or broke the rules (see the Terms below).

Moderation

Who checks acha kaam?

The Acha Karo team. We check that the photo matches the acha kaam and that it's real.

What gets rejected?

  • Photos copied from the internet or someone else.
  • The same photo submitted twice.
  • Staged or fake acha kaam.
  • Anything showing another person's face or private details without their permission.
  • Anything unsafe, hateful or inappropriate.

Should I show people I helped?

Please don't. Respect people's dignity: photograph the acha kaam, not the person receiving help, and never show a child's face.

Still have a question?

Question, bug, or just want to say hi? Our team reads every message.

We only use your name and email to reply to you. We never add you to a mailing list or share your details.
