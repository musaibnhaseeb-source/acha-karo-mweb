How the app works

How does Acha Karo work?
  1. Sign up and complete your profile
  2. Pick an acha kaam and do it
  3. Take a photo of it in the app
  4. It's approved (most right away)
  5. Points land on your profile

There are 32 kinds of acha kaam across 5 areas: Environmental Action, Animal Welfare, Civic & Community, Social & Human Welfare, and Health & Hygiene.

How do I log in? I forgot my password.

No password needed. Enter your email and we'll send you a 6-digit code. It works the same way whether you're new or coming back.

Can't find the code?

  • Check your spam or promotions folder.
  • Wait a minute, then tap Didn't get it? Resend code.
  • Codes expire after a short while, so always use the newest one.

Lost access to that email? Contact us and we'll help.

Does a person check every acha kaam?

Most acha kaam are approved as soon as you submit them, and your points appear straight away.

Some go to our review team first:

  • all donations
  • when your phone couldn't share its location
  • when you do the same kind of acha kaam 3 or more times in one day

These are usually checked within 48 hours.

Why can't I submit another acha kaam right away?

There's a short pause between submissions. You can do each kind of acha kaam once an hour, and there's a 15-minute pause after any submission. During some campaigns the pause is shorter.

Who can use Acha Karo?

Anyone aged 14 or older. One account per person. Under 18? Please use Acha Karo with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

Installing the app

Why isn't Acha Karo on the Play Store?

Not yet. For now you install it straight from our website (this is called an APK). On iPhone, use the web version. It's the same app and works in Safari.

Chrome says "file may be harmful". Is it safe?

Yes. Chrome shows this for every app that doesn't come from the Play Store. It doesn't mean anything was found in our file. Tap Download anyway.

Only download Acha Karo from this website or the link in our Instagram/TikTok bio. Never install a copy someone sends you on WhatsApp or anywhere else.

Want to check it yourself? See How can I check the app is genuine?

What does "Install unknown apps" mean?

Android asks you once to let Chrome install apps from outside the Play Store. Turn it on for Chrome, install Acha Karo, and then you can turn it off again: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Install unknown apps.

Play Protect says the app is unrecognised. What do I do?

Play Protect shows this for new apps that aren't on the Play Store yet. If Play Protect asks, tap More details → Install anyway.

The download button doesn't work from Instagram or TikTok.

Their built-in browsers can't download apps. Tap ⋯ (top right) and choose Open in browser: Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone. Or tap Copy link and paste it into Chrome.

I have an iPhone.

Open our website in Safari and tap Use on iPhone (Web). For an app icon, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

How do I update the app?

Download the latest APK from our website and install it over the old one. Your account, points and badges are saved to your account, not your phone, so nothing is lost.

Points

What are Acha Karo points?

Points show the acha kaam you've done. They're in-app points only, have no cash value, and no purchase is ever needed. You can't sell, swap, transfer or cash them out.

How many points do I get?

Between 8 and 40 points per acha kaam, depending on the effort. For example, watering a plant gives 10, clearing litter gives 15, feeding a stray gives 20, planting a sapling gives 25, and getting a stray neutered gives 40.

My acha kaam is still pending. Why?

It's waiting for our team to check it, usually within 48 hours. If it isn't approved, just do another acha kaam with a clear photo.

Can I lose points?

Yes, if an acha kaam turns out to be fake or copied, or breaks the rules. See the Terms below.

Tiers & badges

What are badges?

There are 40 badges for milestones. For example:

  • your first acha kaam
  • a 7-day streak
  • feeding 5 strays
  • trying all 5 areas
  • reaching the top 10

Each badge unlocks automatically when you get there.

Guddi

Who is Guddi?

Guddi is Acha Karo's mascot, a Pakistani girl who loves doing acha kaam and gets everyone around her to join in. You'll see her in the app and on our Instagram and TikTok.

Why does Guddi show up in the app?

She's there to help, not to nag. Guddi shares a daily acha kaam idea on your Home screen, cheers you on when you do one, and celebrates when you reach a new tier.

She can also send one gentle reminder a day. You can switch it on or off in Settings.

What does "Guddi kuch bhi ban sakti hai" mean?

"Guddi can be anything." She shows up as a soldier, a pilot, a teacher, a sports star and more, because girls in Pakistan can be anything they want to be. It's about strength and ability. And the same goes for you: aap bhi kuch bhi ban sakte hain.

Privacy

What information do you collect?
  • Profile: name, gender, date of birth and city (required). Phone number is optional.
  • Profile photo (optional): stays on your phone and is never uploaded.
  • Acha kaam photos: uploaded so we can check them.
  • Location: when you submit an acha kaam, your phone's location at that moment is saved with it, so we can check it's real.
Who can see my information?

On the leaderboard, other users see your first name and last initial (for example "Ayesha K."), your city, your points and your number of badges.

You don't have to use your real name. A nickname is fine.

Your acha kaam photos and locations are seen only by our review team, never by other users. We never sell your data, and we don't run ads.

What do you do with acha kaam locations?

We use them to check that an acha kaam is real. We may also use them to find areas that need help, and share area-level summaries with partner NGOs. We never share your name, your profile or your exact location.

Locations are kept while your account exists, and deleted when your account is deleted.

Will you post my acha kaam photo on social media?

Only with your permission. We'll ask you first.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account and tell us why. Our team deletes your account within 30 days. Your acha kaam, photos and locations are deleted along with it.

What happens to messages I send through the contact form?

We use your name and email only to reply to you, and we keep messages for 3 months. We never add you to a mailing list.

Moderation

What gets rejected?
  • Photos copied from the internet or from someone else.
  • The same photo submitted twice.
  • Staged or fake acha kaam.
  • Photos showing another person's face or private details without their permission.
  • Anything unsafe, hateful or inappropriate.
Should I show the people I helped?

Please don't. Photograph the acha kaam, not the person (kaam ki tasveer lein, insaan ki nahi). Never show a child's face.

My acha kaam was rejected. What now?

No problem. Just do another one, with a clear photo of the acha kaam itself.

Still have a question?

Question, bug, or just want to say hi? Our team reads every message.

We only use your name and email to reply to you, and we keep messages for 3 months. We never add you to a mailing list or share your details.
