Delete your Acha Karo account

You can ask us to delete your Acha Karo account and its data at any time, from inside the app or from this page. We complete every request within 30 days.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Acha Karo and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Tell us why, if you'd like to, and confirm.

Option 2: Without the app

  1. From the email address you use to log in to Acha Karo, email achakaro.pk@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Acha Karo account". Include your account email, and a reason if you'd like to.
  3. We'll reply to confirm it's really you, then delete your account within 30 days.

✉️ Email a deletion request

If the button doesn't open your email app, send the email yourself to the address above.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login email
  • Your profile: name, city, gender, date of birth and phone number
  • All your acha kaam, including their photos and locations
  • Your points and badges, and your place on the leaderboard
  • Your friends and invites, and the feedback you've sent from the app

What we keep, and for how long

  • A record of your deletion request (the email address and date), so we can show the request was handled. We keep it for 1 year, then delete it.
  • Contact form messages you sent through our website are kept for up to 3 months, then deleted.
  • Moderation records: none are kept. They are deleted along with your account.

Everything else is deleted within 30 days of your request.

Questions?

Email achakaro.pk@gmail.com, or read our Privacy Policy.
