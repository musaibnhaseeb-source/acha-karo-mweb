Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Acha Karo app (Android and the iPhone/web app) and this website, achakaro.vercel.app. It explains what we collect, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version

  • Other users see only your first name and last initial, your city, points, badges and leaderboard rank.
  • Your acha kaam photos and locations are seen only by you and Acha Karo moderators.
  • No ads. No analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app. We never sell your personal data or share it with third parties. Partner organisations only ever see anonymous area totals.
  • You can delete your account at any time. We complete deletion within 30 days.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address. You log in with a one-time code sent to your email. We don't use passwords.

Your profile

  • Name. Other users see only your first name and last initial (for example "Ayesha K."). A nickname is fine.
  • City. Shown publicly next to your name.
  • Gender and date of birth. Used to check you're 14 or older. Not shown to other users.
  • Phone number (optional). Not shown to other users.
  • Profile photo (optional). Stays on your phone and is never uploaded, so we never see it.

Your acha kaam

  • A photo of each acha kaam. Kept in private storage, seen only by you and Acha Karo moderators. The app removes the photo's hidden details (metadata, including GPS) before it is uploaded.
  • Details of each acha kaam: its type, the points it earned and its review status.
  • Location (optional). See Location below.

Other things you send us

  • Feedback you send from inside the app.
  • Account deletion requests, including the reason if you give one.
  • Friends and invites. If you use these features, we store who you've connected with and the invites you've sent or accepted.

What we don't collect

We don't track your location in the background, read your contacts, show ads, or use advertising IDs. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Who can see what

  • Everyone using Acha Karo: your first name and last initial, city, points, badges and leaderboard rank.
  • Only you and Acha Karo moderators: your acha kaam photos and their locations.
  • Only the Acha Karo team: your email, phone number, gender and date of birth, feedback and deletion requests. We use them to run the app, check ages, review acha kaam and reply to you.

We'll only post an acha kaam photo on social media if you give us permission first.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it with third parties.

Anonymous area totals

We may share anonymous, area-level totals with partner organisations such as NGOs — for example, how many acha kaam were done in a neighbourhood. This never includes names, emails, phone numbers, photos or exact locations, and an area is only included when at least 5 different members were active there.

Location

  • Sharing your location is optional. The app asks you before each acha kaam.
  • If you agree, the app records an approximate location (about 100 metres) only at the moment you submit. It never tracks you in the background.
  • We use it to help check the acha kaam is real. Only you and Acha Karo moderators can see it.

How we store and protect it

  • Your data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database service, and is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Acha kaam photos are kept in private storage, not public links.
  • We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it as described below.

Fonts

The app and this website load their fonts from Google Fonts. When the fonts load, Google can see your device's IP address, as with any website request. We don't send Google anything else.

This website

  • Hosting. The website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives standard request information (such as your IP address and browser type) when you visit.
  • Visit counts. Our home page uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally. This page, the Help page and the account deletion page don't use it.
  • Live stats. The home page reads the total number of acha kaam from our database to show it. It sends nothing about you.
  • Contact form. The form on our Help page sends your name, email and message to our Supabase database, where the Acha Karo team reads it. We use your details only to reply, keep messages for 3 months, and never add you to a mailing list.

The website doesn't set cookies or ask for your location.

Age

Acha Karo is for people 14 or older. If you're under 18, please use Acha Karo with a parent or guardian's knowledge. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 14, we'll delete it.

Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

We complete deletion within 30 days. Your account, profile, acha kaam, photos, locations, points and badges are deleted, and you're removed from the leaderboard. The deletion page lists the few things we keep afterwards, why, and for how long.

To ask for a copy of your data, or to correct it, email us at the address below.
